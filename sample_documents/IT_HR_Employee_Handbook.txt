# IT and HR Employee Handbook

## 1. Purpose

This handbook explains the company's employee policies,
working arrangements, leave rules, IT security standards,
and workplace responsibilities.

It applies to all permanent employees and approved
contractors unless a specific employment agreement
states otherwise.

## 2. Working Hours and Attendance

Standard working hours are Monday to Friday,
9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Employees are expected to:
- Complete 8 working hours per day.
- Follow their assigned shift timings.
- Mark attendance through the HR portal.
- Inform their manager about unexpected delays.
- Obtain approval before changing scheduled shifts.

Employees working in support operations may have
different shift schedules depending on business needs.

## 3. Work From Home Policy

Employees may work remotely with manager approval.

Guidelines:
- Remote work must be requested through the HR portal.
- Employees must remain available during working hours.
- Company VPN must be used when accessing internal systems.
- Confidential company information must not be stored
  on personal devices.
- Managers may request office attendance for team meetings.

## 4. Leave Policy

Employees can apply for leave through the HR portal.

### 4.1 Annual Leave

Employees receive 24 days of annual paid leave
per calendar year.

Planned leave should be submitted at least
5 business days in advance.

### 4.2 Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 days of sick leave per year.

For absence exceeding 2 consecutive working days,
a medical certificate may be requested by HR.

### 4.3 Emergency Leave

Employees may request emergency leave for
unexpected personal or family situations.

The employee should inform their manager as soon
as reasonably possible.

### 4.4 Leave Approval

The reporting manager reviews leave requests
based on team availability and project requirements.

HR maintains the official leave balance.

## 5. IT Equipment and Asset Management

The company may provide:
- Laptop
- Monitor
- Keyboard and mouse
- Company mobile phone
- VPN access
- Other approved IT equipment

Employees must:
- Keep company equipment secure.
- Avoid installing unauthorised software.
- Report damaged or lost devices to IT.
- Return all company assets during exit clearance.

## 6. Password and Account Security

Employees must follow these security requirements:

- Use unique passwords for company accounts.
- Enable multi-factor authentication.
- Never share passwords with colleagues.
- Never store credentials in source code.
- Lock laptops when away from their desks.
- Report suspicious login attempts immediately.

IT administrators will periodically review
user access permissions.

## 7. Data Protection

Company information is classified into four categories:

| Classification | Description |
|---|---|
| Public | Approved for external sharing |
| Internal | For employee use |
| Confidential | Sensitive business information |
| Restricted | Credentials and highly sensitive information |

Employees must only access information
required for their job responsibilities.

Confidential and restricted information must not
be shared through personal email or public
file-sharing platforms.

## 8. IT Support and Incident Reporting

Employees should create a service desk ticket
for technical issues.

Examples:
- Laptop not working
- VPN connection failure
- Password reset
- Application access issue
- Email connectivity problem

Critical production incidents must be reported
immediately to the IT operations team.

The ticket should include:
- Employee ID
- Application or system name
- Error description
- Time of occurrence
- Screenshot, if appropriate

Passwords and authentication tokens must never
be included in support tickets.

## 9. Performance and Appraisal

Employee performance is reviewed annually.

The review considers:
- Technical skills
- Quality of work
- Delivery commitments
- Team collaboration
- Communication
- Learning and development

Managers provide feedback and identify
development opportunities.

Salary revisions are subject to company policy,
performance, business conditions, and approval.

## 10. Training and Development

Employees are encouraged to participate in
technical and professional training.

Training requests must be approved by the
reporting manager.

Employees attending company-sponsored training
may be required to complete assigned assessments.

## 11. Code of Conduct

All employees are expected to:
- Treat colleagues respectfully.
- Maintain professional communication.
- Protect confidential information.
- Avoid conflicts of interest.
- Follow company policies.
- Report unethical or inappropriate behaviour.

## 12. Resignation and Exit Process

Employees planning to resign must submit
a formal resignation through the HR portal.

The notice period is determined by the
employment agreement.

Before the final working day, employees must:
- Complete knowledge transfer.
- Return company equipment.
- Close pending service requests.
- Complete project handover.
- Obtain IT and HR clearance.

Final settlement is processed according
to company policy and applicable law.

## 13. Policy Exceptions

Any exception to this handbook requires
documented approval from the relevant
department head and HR.

The company may revise these policies
when business or regulatory requirements change.
